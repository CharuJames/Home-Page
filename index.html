Charu Karakkaparambil James

PhD Researcher in Informatics | Natural Language Processing & Machine Learning

I am a PhD researcher in Informatics at RPTU Kaiserslautern–Landau. My research focuses on neural topic modelling and natural language processing, particularly how topics evolve over time and across languages.

I develop and evaluate methods for continual learning, cross-lingual knowledge distillation, and multilingual topic modelling, with an emphasis on low-resource languages. I also investigate how large language models can support more reliable evaluation of topic coherence and semantic change.

Previously, I worked as a research assistant at RPTU. I hold a master’s degree in Informatics, specialising in Intelligent Systems, from TU Kaiserslautern, and a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Cochin University of Science and Technology.
